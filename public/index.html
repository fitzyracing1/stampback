Stampback

A stamp that writes back.

Stampback puts a scannable stamp on real mail and sends the reply back to your desk. When someone scans it they tap got it, opened and read, I'll take care of it or sending it back. If nobody scans it before your deadline, the desk says not received.

No account. Stampback does not email anyone: replies stay on your desk.

scan to reply
1 · Start a deskYour desk is a private link. Open it on your phone with “Copy desk link”.
2 · Name the pieceA private nickname (“Mom's birthday card”), never an address. Pick 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.
3 · Print & stickPrint one stamp or a sheet of up to eight. Stick the QR on the envelope.
4 · Hear backThe reply shows up on your desk. “Alert me” notifies you while it's open.
